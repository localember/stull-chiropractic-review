FAQ

Common Questions About Chiropractic Care

Answers to some of the questions we hear most from patients in Kettering.

Ask us directly
What happens at a first visit?
We take a full history, talk through what you are feeling and when it started, then examine before anything else happens. You will hear what we found and what we recommend before you decide on care.
Do I need a referral to come in?
No. You can book directly with our office.
Are all patients adjusted the same way?
No. The approach is chosen for your body, your history and your comfort, and it changes as you progress.
Are chiropractic adjustments safe?
Becoming a chiropractor takes years of doctoral study and supervised clinical training. Gentle, precise adjustments are the core of the work, aimed at restoring alignment so the nerves and muscles can do their job.
What is MLS laser therapy?
A focused light applied over the sore area to settle inflammation and ease pain. It does not hurt, and there is no recovery time afterwards.
How does a chiropractor help with sciatica?
By looking for what is compressing or irritating the nerve rather than only easing the pain, then addressing that cause.
